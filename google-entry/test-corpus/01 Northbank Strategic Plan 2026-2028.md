## Our four commitments

**1. Bring care closer to home.** By 2028, one in five outpatient episodes will be delivered in the community or at home. We will fund two mobile clinics, extend telehealth to every specialty that can safely use it, and partner with the three councils in our catchment on shared community rooms.

**2. One digital front door.** Patients and carers will book, see their record and message their care team through one experience. We will retire the four separate booking lines by the end of 2027 and measure patient effort every quarter.

**3. Grow and keep our rural workforce.** Our three rural sites carry vacancy rates above 18%. We will recruit through a rural training pathway with the regional university, offer accommodation support, and target 12-month retention of 85% by 2028.

**4. Financial sustainability.** We will return to a balanced operating result by FY28 without cutting services, through activity-based funding capture, procurement savings and a review of under-used facilities.

## How we will know

Each commitment has a success signal reported to the Board quarterly. The Board's Strategy Committee owns the drift review.

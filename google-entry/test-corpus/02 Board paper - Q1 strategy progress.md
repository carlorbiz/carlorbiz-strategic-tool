## Care closer to home

The first mobile clinic began operating in August and ran 412 episodes in its first eight weeks, 94% of which would otherwise have been hospital outpatient visits. Telehealth now covers nine specialties, up from five. Community and home episodes were 11.4% of total outpatient activity for the quarter, against a baseline of 8.1%. The second mobile clinic is delayed to March because the vehicle fit-out contractor withdrew.

## One digital front door

The booking platform procurement closed with a preferred vendor. Contract signing is expected in November. Two of the four legacy booking lines have been mapped for retirement. Patient effort was not measured this quarter; the survey instrument is still in design.

## Rural workforce

Vacancy at the three rural sites rose to 21% following four resignations at Marlow. Exit interviews cite accommodation cost and on-call load. The university pathway has its first cohort of six students, who will not be in the workforce before 2028. The Executive regards this as the highest risk to the plan and asks the Board to note that the FY27 agency staffing budget will be exceeded.
